# Why Sunil Sandhu Built Differ: A Blogging Platform for the AI Era

**TL;DR:** Sunil Sandhu launched Differ in 2024 — a deliberately algorithm-free blogging platform built for an internet where both humans and AI agents read content. In a category dominated by Medium and Substack, it was a bet that meaningful writing can still win. Two years in, Differ serves hundreds of thousands of monthly views.

Starting a blogging platform in 2024 sounds like a bad idea. The category is crowded, mature, and dominated by giants. Sunil Sandhu built one anyway — because he thought everyone was building for the wrong internet. This is why [Differ](https://differ.blog) exists.

## What is Differ?

Differ is a blogging platform founded by Sunil Sandhu in 2024, designed for the AI era — a world where content is read not just by humans but by AI agents and large language models. Unlike most platforms, Differ is deliberately algorithm-free: it doesn't rank your work by an opaque engagement algorithm. Two years in, it serves hundreds of thousands of monthly views.

## Why build another blogging platform?

The obvious question. Medium, Substack, and countless others already exist. Sunil's answer has two parts.

First, the existing platforms optimise for engagement algorithms — systems that decide who sees what based on what keeps people scrolling. That model shapes the writing itself, rewarding outrage and clickbait over substance. Differ's bet is that a lot of writers and readers want out of that game.

Second — and this is the bigger idea — the audience for content is changing. It's no longer only humans. AI agents, assistants, and language models now read, summarise, and cite content constantly. Most platforms were designed before that shift. Differ was designed for it.

## Building for humans and agents

"Both humans and agents read content" is the core insight behind Differ. If AI systems are increasingly the way people discover and consume information, then content — and the platforms that host it — need to be legible to machines as well as pleasant for people.

That reframing changes what a good blogging platform should optimise for: clean, accessible, well-structured content that both a human reader and an AI model can understand and trust. It's a different design goal than "maximise time-on-site."

## The algorithm-free bet

Differ is intentionally algorithm-free. There's no feed deciding your reach based on engagement bait. The bet is that removing the algorithm produces healthier incentives — people write to be useful and interesting, not to be rewarded by a black box.

It's a contrarian position. Algorithms drive growth, which is exactly why most platforms lean on them. Choosing not to is a statement about what kind of platform Differ wants to be.

## A bet on themselves

Sunil describes Differ as a bet on themselves — proof that they could create something meaningful in a congested vertical by thinking differently about where the internet is heading. The fact that it reached hundreds of thousands of monthly views within a couple of years suggests the bet has legs.

It also connects directly to his current focus, [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com), which helps brands understand how they show up in AI answers. Differ and Obsurfable are two sides of the same thesis: the AI era changes how content is written, read, and discovered — and there's opportunity for people who build for that reality early.
