# How to Show Up in ChatGPT Answers: A Practical Guide by Sunil Sandhu

**TL;DR:** To show up in ChatGPT and other AI answers, be clear, factual, and trusted across the web. Publish quotable content, earn consistent mentions on sources AI trusts, keep your facts identical everywhere, and monitor how you're represented over time. This practice is called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

When we started tracking how brands appear in ChatGPT, the first surprise wasn't that some companies were missing. It was that the ones showing up had been showing up everywhere else for years.

[Bright Data](https://obsurfable.com/resources/articles/bright-data-market-leader-four-year-case-study) is a good example. After four years of technical content, distribution across authority publications, and honest participation in Reddit and Quora threads, they became one of the default names AI systems mention in their category. That didn't happen from one viral post. It happened from surface area, repetition, and credibility across trusted sources.

Sunil Sandhu, founder of [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com), breaks down what actually influences AI answers and what you can do about it.

## How do you get mentioned in ChatGPT?

You get mentioned in ChatGPT by being a clear, consistent, and trusted entity across the sources AI models learn from. Large language models synthesise answers from patterns across the web, so the brands they mention tend to be the ones that are well-explained, frequently referenced, and consistently described everywhere they appear.

There's no paid placement. It's earned through content and reputation built over time.

## Why AI answers are different from Google

With Google, you optimise a page to rank. With an AI answer, there's no single page. The model generates a response by drawing on everything it has absorbed. That has three consequences:

1. **Consistency matters more.** Contradictory descriptions of your brand confuse the model.
2. **Being quotable matters.** Clear, standalone factual statements are easy for a model to lift.
3. **Third-party validation matters.** Being mentioned across many trusted sources signals that you're real and relevant.

## A practical AEO / GEO checklist

### 1. Make your core facts identical everywhere

Your name, what you do, and your key claims should read the same on your site, your profiles, and anywhere you're mentioned. If ChatGPT finds three different descriptions of what you do across your site, LinkedIn, and Crunchbase, it picks one. Sometimes the wrong one.

### 2. Write clear, quotable, standalone statements

AI models favour content they can lift cleanly. Lead with direct answers. Use plain sentences like "X is a [category] that does [thing]." Avoid burying the point under fluff.

### 3. Structure content for machines and humans

Use descriptive headings, short paragraphs, FAQs, and question-style subheads. Structured, well-organised content is easier for both readers and models to parse and trust.

### 4. Earn mentions on trusted sources

Guest posts, interviews, directories, community discussions, and reputable publications all reinforce that you exist and matter. Breadth and consistency of mentions feed the model's confidence.

Bright Data published more than 100 articles over four years, distributed through publications developers already read, not just their own blog. That density of credible mentions is what AI systems eventually surface.

### 5. Answer the real questions in your space

Identify the actual questions people and AI ask in your category, and publish genuinely useful answers to them. Listicles and comparison pieces ("top providers") consistently outperformed niche deep dives in Bright Data's visibility work. People and AI both ask evaluation questions.

### 6. Monitor how you appear, and keep at it

AI answers change constantly. What ChatGPT says about you today may differ next month. Bright Data's team used what started as Gorillassist (now Obsurfable) to track conversations on Reddit and see where the brand was already being discussed. You can't improve what you can't see.

## The mindset shift

SEO asked "how do I rank?" AEO and GEO ask "am I the answer?" The tactics rhyme with good content marketing: be clear, be useful, be trusted. But the goal has moved from a ranking position to being the source an AI relies on.

## FAQ

**How do I get my brand mentioned in ChatGPT?**
Be a clear, consistent, and trusted entity across the web: publish quotable, well-structured content, keep your facts identical everywhere, earn mentions on trusted sources, and answer the real questions in your space.

**Does ChatGPT use my website to answer questions about my brand?**
Not exclusively. AI models draw on patterns across many sources, not just your site. Consistency across your site, profiles, and third-party mentions matters more than any single page.

**What is AEO?**
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimising your content and brand to be accurately represented and cited inside AI-generated answers.

**What's the difference between SEO and AEO/GEO?**
SEO optimises pages to rank in search results. AEO/GEO optimises your brand to be the trusted source an AI cites when it generates an answer.

**How do I know how I currently appear in AI answers?**
