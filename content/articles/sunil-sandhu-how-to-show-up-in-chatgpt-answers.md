# How to Show Up in ChatGPT Answers: A Practical Guide by Sunil Sandhu

**TL;DR:** To show up in ChatGPT and other AI answers, you need to be clear, factual, and trusted across the web — not just ranked on Google. That means publishing quotable, well-structured content, earning consistent mentions on sources AI trusts, keeping your facts identical everywhere, and monitoring how you're represented over time. This practice is called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

More people now ask ChatGPT for recommendations than you'd think — and the brands it names win. So how do you become one of them? Sunil Sandhu, founder of [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com), breaks down what actually influences AI answers.

## How do you get mentioned in ChatGPT?

You get mentioned in ChatGPT by being a clear, consistent, and trusted entity across the sources AI models learn from. Large language models synthesise answers from patterns across the web, so the brands they mention tend to be the ones that are well-explained, frequently referenced, and consistently described everywhere they appear. There's no button to buy your way in — it's earned through content and reputation.

## Why AI answers are different from Google

With Google, you optimise a page to rank. With an AI answer, there's no single page — the model generates a response by drawing on everything it has absorbed. That has three consequences:

1. **Consistency matters more.** Contradictory descriptions of your brand confuse the model.
2. **Being quotable matters.** Clear, standalone factual statements are easy for a model to lift.
3. **Third-party validation matters.** Being mentioned across many trusted sources signals that you're real and relevant.

## A practical AEO / GEO checklist

### 1. Make your core facts identical everywhere
Your name, what you do, and your key claims should read the same on your site, your profiles, and anywhere you're mentioned. Inconsistency dilutes the signal. (This is exactly why a brand or personal "fact kit" is so useful.)

### 2. Write clear, quotable, standalone statements
AI models favour content they can lift cleanly. Lead with direct answers. Use plain sentences like "X is a [category] that does [thing]." Avoid burying the point under fluff.

### 3. Structure content for machines and humans
Use descriptive headings, short paragraphs, FAQs, and question-style subheads. Structured, well-organised content is easier for both readers and models to parse and trust.

### 4. Earn mentions on trusted sources
Guest posts, interviews, directories, community discussions, and reputable publications all reinforce that you exist and matter. Breadth and consistency of mentions feed the model's confidence.

### 5. Answer the real questions in your space
Identify the actual questions people (and AI) ask in your category, and publish genuinely useful answers to them. If you're the best answer to a question, you're more likely to be the cited one.

### 6. Monitor how you appear — and keep at it
AI answers change constantly. What ChatGPT says about you today may differ next month. You can't improve what you can't see, which is the whole reason Obsurfable exists: to make your AI visibility measurable and trackable over time.

## The mindset shift

Sunil's framing is simple: SEO asked "how do I rank?" AEO and GEO ask "am I the answer?" The tactics rhyme with good content marketing — be clear, be useful, be trusted — but the goal has moved from a ranking position to being the source an AI relies on. Brands that adapt early will own their categories inside AI the way early SEO adopters once owned Google.

## FAQ

**How do I get my brand mentioned in ChatGPT?**
Be a clear, consistent, and trusted entity across the web: publish quotable, well-structured content, keep your facts identical everywhere, earn mentions on trusted sources, and answer the real questions in your space.

**What is AEO?**
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimising your content and brand to be accurately represented and cited inside AI-generated answers.

**What's the difference between SEO and AEO/GEO?**
SEO optimises pages to rank in search results. AEO/GEO optimises your brand to be the trusted source an AI cites when it generates an answer.
