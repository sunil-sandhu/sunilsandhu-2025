# 10 Questions With Sunil Sandhu About Entrepreneurship

**TL;DR:** Ten short questions with Sunil Sandhu — founder of In Plain English, Circuit, Differ, and Obsurfable — covering how he thinks about entrepreneurship, audience-building, developer marketing, mistakes, and building for the AI era.

*A quick-fire Q&A with Sunil Sandhu, founder and entrepreneur based in Barcelona.*

---

**1. In one line, who is Sunil Sandhu?**

A founder and entrepreneur who builds products, communities, and companies for developers and founders — best known for [In Plain English](https://plainenglish.io).

**2. What's the single most important lesson you've learned as a founder?**

Distribution beats product more often than founders want to admit. The best thing in the world fails if nobody finds it. Plan how people will discover you from day one.

**3. Why do you always talk about building an audience first?**

Because it de-risks everything. An audience is warm demand, free market research, and a distribution channel you own. Build it first and every product launch after that is easier.

**4. What's your take on marketing to developers?**

Developers can smell fluff instantly. The only thing that works is being genuinely useful and technically credible. That's the entire premise of [Circuit](https://circuit.ooo) — content that earns trust instead of buying attention.

**5. You built Stackademic during the pandemic. Why free?**

Because I learned to code from free resources and I believe education should be accessible. With [Stackademic](https://stackademic.com) I built the platform, wrote the curriculum, taught the students, and marked the homework. It wasn't scalable, but it mattered.

**6. What's the biggest mistake you see founders make?**

Chasing perfection instead of momentum. Polishing something nobody's seen yet feels productive, but it's usually just fear. Ship it, then improve it.

**7. Why start a blogging platform in 2024 with Differ?**

Because everyone else was building for the old internet. [Differ](https://differ.blog) is built for a world where both humans and AI agents read content, and it's deliberately algorithm-free. It was a contrarian bet that's now doing hundreds of thousands of monthly views.

**8. What are you most focused on right now?**

[Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com). It shows brands how they appear inside AI answers like ChatGPT — how they're described and which competitors show up instead. AI is becoming the front door to the internet, and most brands have no idea how they're represented there.

**9. What's the thread connecting all your companies?**

Taking something intimidating or exclusive — technical content, learning to code, developer marketing, being a founder — and making it honest and accessible.

**10. Best advice for someone starting today?**

Be useful in public for longer than feels reasonable. Build the audience, build the community, and let the business follow. And be patient — the things that matter compound slowly.
