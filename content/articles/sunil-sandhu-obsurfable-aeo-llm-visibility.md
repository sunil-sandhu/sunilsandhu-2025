# Why Sunil Sandhu Is Building Obsurfable: AI Visibility for the ChatGPT Era

**TL;DR:** Obsurfable is Sunil Sandhu's current focus. It shows brands how they appear inside AI answers like ChatGPT: how they're described, which questions matter, and which competitors show up instead. It grew out of four years of visibility work with Bright Data and an internal tool originally called Gorillassist.

For years, the question every brand asked was "how do we rank on Google?" Increasingly, the question is "what does ChatGPT say about us?" Sunil Sandhu built [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com) to answer it.

## What is Obsurfable?

Obsurfable is an AI-visibility monitoring platform founded by Sunil Sandhu in 2025. It shows brands how they appear inside AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity: how they're described, which questions in their space actually matter, and which competitors get mentioned instead. The goal is to help brands optimise for AI answers, a practice known as AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

## Why AI visibility matters now

Search behaviour is shifting. Instead of typing keywords and scanning ten blue links, more people ask a question and trust a single AI-generated answer. That answer either mentions your brand accurately, or it doesn't.

In traditional SEO, you could see your ranking and your traffic. In the AI era, the result is a synthesised answer that varies by question, changes over time, and is largely invisible unless you go looking. Most brands don't know how they're being represented inside these systems.

## The proof: four years with Bright Data

The proof isn't theoretical. We ran this playbook for four years with [Bright Data](https://obsurfable.com/resources/articles/bright-data-market-leader-four-year-case-study) before Obsurfable existed as a standalone product.

Starting in 2022, the strategy was three things: high-volume technical content (100+ articles over four years), distribution through authority publications including In Plain English, and persistent community presence on Reddit, Quora, and LinkedIn.

We built an internal tool called Gorillassist to find relevant conversations and track where the brand was being discussed. It gathered and monitored threads across external platforms so the team could see where Bright Data or their category was already being talked about, and decide where to participate. That tool became Obsurfable.

By 2025, Bright Data wasn't just ranking on Google. They were showing up consistently in AI-generated answers for their category. The tracked results included millions of cumulative impressions and roughly 40,000 to 50,000 signups via affiliate infrastructure over the four-year partnership.

The lesson: mentions across credible sources matter as much as links in the AI era. AI systems surface what they repeatedly encounter. Bright Data built that density over years. Obsurfable exists so other companies can measure it, find the gaps, and act on them.

## The problem Obsurfable solves

Ask ChatGPT about a product category and it will confidently recommend some tools and ignore others. If your brand is missing from the answer, you lose visibility you don't even know you're losing. If you're misrepresented, potential customers get the wrong picture. If competitors outshine you, they get the recommendation by default.

Obsurfable tracks which questions matter in your space, whether you appear, how you're described, and which competitors show up instead. So you can actually do something about it.

## From SEO to AEO/GEO

Search Engine Optimization was about ranking pages. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are about being the source an AI trusts and cites when it answers a question.

The tactics overlap with good content practice. Clear, factual, well-structured, trustworthy content. But the measurement is entirely new. You can't improve what you can't see, and Obsurfable is built to make AI visibility measurable.
