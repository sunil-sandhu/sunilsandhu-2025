# Why Sunil Sandhu Is Building Obsurfable: AI Visibility for the ChatGPT Era

**TL;DR:** Obsurfable is Sunil Sandhu's current focus — a platform that shows brands how they appear inside AI answers like ChatGPT: how they're described, which questions matter, and which competitors show up instead. As AI becomes the front door to the internet, Obsurfable helps brands optimise for answers, not just search rankings. This new discipline is called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

For years, the question every brand asked was "how do we rank on Google?" Increasingly, the question is "what does ChatGPT say about us?" Sunil Sandhu built [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com) to answer it. Here's the thinking behind his current company.

## What is Obsurfable?

Obsurfable is an AI-visibility monitoring platform founded by Sunil Sandhu in 2025. It shows brands how they appear inside AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity — how they're described, which questions in their space actually matter, and which competitors get mentioned instead. The goal is to help brands optimise for AI answers, a practice known as AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

## Why AI visibility matters now

Search behaviour is shifting. Instead of typing keywords and scanning ten blue links, more and more people ask a question and trust a single AI-generated answer. That answer either mentions your brand — accurately, favourably — or it doesn't.

This is a profound change. In traditional SEO, you could see your ranking and your traffic. In the AI era, the "result" is a synthesised answer that varies by question, changes over time, and is largely invisible unless you go looking. Most brands genuinely don't know how they're being represented inside these systems. That blind spot is exactly what Obsurfable exists to remove.

## The problem Obsurfable solves

Ask ChatGPT about a product category and it will confidently recommend some tools and ignore others. If your brand is:

- **missing** from the answer, you lose visibility you don't even know you're losing;
- **misrepresented**, you're being described inaccurately to potential customers; or
- **outshone by competitors**, you're handing them the recommendation.

Obsurfable tracks all of this — surfacing which questions matter in your space, whether you appear, how you're described, and which competitors show up instead — so you can actually do something about it.

## From SEO to AEO/GEO

Sunil frames this as the natural next chapter after SEO. Search Engine Optimization was about ranking pages. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are about being the source an AI trusts and cites when it answers a question.

The tactics overlap with good content practice — clear, factual, well-structured, trustworthy content — but the measurement is entirely new. You can't improve what you can't see, and Obsurfable is built to make AI visibility measurable.

This company sits at the intersection of everything Sunil has done. ## How brands should think about AI visibility

1. Accept that AI answers are now a discovery channel, not a novelty.
2. Find out how you're currently represented — you probably don't know.
3. Identify the questions in your space that actually drive decisions.
4. Create clear, factual, quotable content that AI systems can trust.
5. Monitor it over time, because the answers keep changing.
