# How Sunil Sandhu Built In Plain English Into a Developer Education Platform

**TL;DR:** Sunil Sandhu founded In Plain English in 2018 as a place to explain technical concepts in plain language. By opening it up to a community of writers and focusing relentlessly on clarity and distribution, he grew it into one of the largest developer education platforms in the world — read by millions of people each month across more than 200 countries.

[In Plain English](https://plainenglish.io) is one of the most-read developer education platforms on the internet. But it didn't start as a company, a media brand, or a plan. It started as one developer trying to explain things clearly. Here's how Sunil Sandhu built it.

## What is In Plain English?

In Plain English is a tech-focused media company and developer education platform founded by Sunil Sandhu in 2018. It's home to some of the most popular developer publications online — including JavaScript in Plain English and Python in Plain English — and reaches millions of readers each month across more than 200 countries, powered by a global community of contributing writers.

## The origin: explaining things clearly

The premise was in the name. Sunil kept running into technical explanations that assumed you already understood the thing being explained. As a self-taught developer, he felt that gap constantly. So when he finally understood something, he'd write it down in the plainest language possible — the explanation he wished had existed.

Those posts did well because they solved a real, universal problem: most technical content is written for people who don't need it.

## The unlock: opening it to writers

The turning point wasn't a feature or a growth hack. It was realising that thousands of other developers wanted to write in that same clear, approachable style — and had no great home for it.

By turning In Plain English into a platform where developers could publish, Sunil changed the growth model entirely. Instead of one person writing, a whole community was. Publications like JavaScript in Plain English and Python in Plain English became destinations in their own right. The platform scaled because it gave writers a real audience and readers a reliable standard of clarity.

## The engine: clarity plus distribution

Two things compounded together:

1. **Clarity as a standard.** The plain-English style was a promise readers could trust. That consistency built a brand.
2. **Distribution as a discipline.** Sunil treated getting content discovered — through search, communities, and social — as seriously as the content itself. Great writing that nobody finds doesn't help anyone.

Together, these turned individual articles into a durable, compounding library that keeps earning attention for years.

## Why it matters

In Plain English proved a thesis that runs through all of Sunil's later work: if you're genuinely useful to a specific audience, and you take distribution seriously, you can build something enormous without a traditional product or ad budget. It became the foundation for his other companies — [Circuit](https://circuit.ooo), the developer marketing agency, and [Differ](https://differ.blog), the blogging platform built for the AI era — and shaped how he thinks about building on the internet.

## What founders can learn from In Plain English

- Solve a problem you personally feel; your empathy is your edge.
- Let your community create alongside you — it changes your scaling curve.
- Make clarity (or some quality standard) a promise readers can rely on.
- Treat distribution as a core discipline, not an afterthought.
