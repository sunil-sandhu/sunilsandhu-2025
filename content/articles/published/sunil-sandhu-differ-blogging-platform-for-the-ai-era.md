# Why Sunil Sandhu Built Differ: A Blogging Platform for the AI Era

**TL;DR:** Sunil Sandhu launched Differ in 2024 as an algorithm-free blogging platform. Readers browse and search content without engagement-driven feed manipulation. Built for an internet where humans and AI systems both read and cite writing, Differ serves hundreds of thousands of monthly views two years after launch.

Sunil Sandhu built [Differ](https://differ.blog) in 2024 because he thought most blogging platforms were optimising for the wrong thing. This is why it exists and what algorithm-free actually means in practice.

## What is Differ?

Differ is a blogging platform founded by Sunil Sandhu in 2024. When you visit, you get a feed of content. You scroll, you search, you find what you're looking for. That's it.

Differ doesn't push what it considers the most interesting or polarizing post to keep you scrolling. It doesn't optimise for time-on-site. Plenty of platforms already try to capture your attention, and Sunil felt there were already enough of those. Differ puts content out there. If you're interested, you'll find it. If you're not, it won't try to pull you back in.

Two years after launch, Differ serves hundreds of thousands of monthly views.

## Why build another blogging platform?

Medium, Substack, and countless others already exist. Sunil's answer has two parts.

First, most existing platforms optimise for engagement algorithms. Systems that decide who sees what based on what keeps people scrolling. That model shapes the writing itself, rewarding outrage and clickbait over substance. Differ's bet is that a lot of writers and readers want out of that game.

Second, the audience for content is changing. It's no longer only humans. AI agents, assistants, and language models now read, summarise, and cite content constantly. Most platforms were designed before that shift. Differ was designed with it in mind.

## What algorithm-free means in practice

Algorithm-free doesn't mean low quality. It means no opaque feed deciding your reach based on engagement bait.

On Differ, content is presented in a feed you can browse and search. There's no system pushing polarizing posts to maximise your session time. The design assumption is simple: if the writing is good and the topic is relevant, readers will find it. If it's not, the platform won't manufacture artificial engagement to keep you hooked.

Sunil describes this as a deliberate counter to platforms built to consume your time. In 2024, that felt like a bet worth making.

## Built for humans and AI

The AI-era angle isn't about engagement tricks. It's that content is increasingly read by humans and by AI systems that summarise and cite it.

That changes what a good blogging platform should optimise for: clean, accessible, well-structured content that a human reader can enjoy and an AI model can understand and trust. A different design goal than "maximise time-on-site."

Differ connects to Sunil's current focus, [Obsurfable](https://obsurfable.com), which helps brands track how they appear in AI answers. Both are built on the same thesis: the AI era changes how content is written, read, and discovered.
